## 📝 SECTION 7: CAPTION FORMULA

### The 4-Part Caption Structure

1. **LINE 1: Hook** *(Grab attention in the first line before "more")*
   > *"The moment she saw him and forgot the whole world existed..."*
2. **LINES 2–4: Story / Emotion**
   > *"This was captured at [location] during [event]. What made this shot special was [detail]. Every frame has a story — this one made us cry happy tears."*
3. **LINE 5: Call to Action (CTA)**
   > *"💌 DM us to book your session | Link in bio"*
4. **LINES 6+: Hashtags** *(First comment OR after dot breaks)*
   > `.`  
   > `.`  
   > `.`  
   > `#hashtags`
